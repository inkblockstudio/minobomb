Minobomb – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Minobomb ("the game") is a mobile game published by Inkblock Studio ("we", "us"). This page explains what the game does with information. The short version: it collects nothing about you.

What we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (coins, upgrades, quest stars and settings) is saved only on your device. If you use Android's backup, Google may back this data up to your Google account under Google's own terms; we cannot see it.

Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases. They are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment details from you; the game only learns from Google Play that a purchase was completed so it can give you the item. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles payments.

Network

The game runs offline and does not send data to any server of ours.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
