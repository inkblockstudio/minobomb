Minobomb – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Minobomb ("the game") is a mobile game published by Inkblock Studio ("we", "us"). This page explains what the game does with information. The short version: we collect nothing about you ourselves. If you choose to watch an optional reward video, Google's ad service is involved, as described below.

What we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Optional reward videos (ads)

The game offers optional reward videos, for example to double the coins you won in a battle. Videos are never forced; you only see one when you tap a button that offers it.

These videos are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as the device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. This information goes to Google, not to us; we only see total numbers (for example, how many videos were shown). Google's use of this data is described in its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices (ads). On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (coins, upgrades, quest stars and settings) is saved only on your device. If you use Android's backup, Google may back this data up to your Google account under Google's own terms; we cannot see it. You can also save a backup file of your progress yourself (Settings → Save backup); the file goes only where you choose to save it.

Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases. They are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment details from you; the game only learns from Google Play that a purchase was completed so it can give you the item. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles payments.

Network

The game runs offline and does not send data to any server of ours. An internet connection is only used by Google Play for purchases and by Google AdMob when reward videos are offered or shown.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
